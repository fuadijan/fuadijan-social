# Fuadijan | 30-Day Social Content Calendar

**Working master calendar · Instagram-first, adaptable to Facebook and TikTok**  
**Dates:** Day 1–30 are sequence placeholders. Add the actual dates once the posting start date is confirmed.  
**Location reference:** 129 Parramatta Rd, Auburn. Use this address consistently after confirming it is current.

## How to use this calendar

- Publish one feed post each day in sequence. If daily posting is not practical, keep the sequence and spread it across five to seven weeks; the story arc still works.
- Film vertical (9:16) for Reels and Stories. Capture clean audio where useful, steady close-ups, and a finished-plate frame. For feed photos/designs, make a 4:5 portrait master and keep text away from the edges.
- Use only dishes, ingredients, preparation claims, reviews, menu items and availability that Fuadijan can confirm. Bracketed notes are production placeholders, not copy to publish.
- Do not invent prices, discounts, limited-time availability, customer names, review wording, recipes or cooking claims. Obtain permission before featuring identifiable customers or quoting a review.
- Suggested CTA and hashtags are included with each caption. Use 3–5 hashtags per post.

## Weekly story arc

| Sequence | Story | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | **Introducing Fuadijan** | Establish the fire-led identity, signature Chapli Kebab and Chicken Karahi, heritage and the joy of sharing. |
| Days 8–14 | **Make Chapli famous** | Explain and dramatise Chapli, show its modern expression in the Shami Burger, introduce the team and Auburn community, then land on a BBQ feast. |
| Days 15–21 | **Modern Fuadijan, rooted in craft** | Make Chicken Karahi a first-visit recommendation, show the craft behind it, broaden the menu story, and build trust through customers and reviews. |
| Days 22–30 | **From the fire to what comes next** | Build curiosity around a new fire-grilled item only if confirmed, add dessert and tradition, feature the team, return to Chapli, invite a weekend visit and customer input, then close on the brand promise. |

## Production checklist

### Before filming
- Confirm the dish is currently on the menu and available on the planned posting date. Confirm correct dish names, ingredients, sides and preparation claims with the kitchen.
- Choose one clean, uncluttered filming area; wipe the lens; clear distracting items; check that food is fresh and the plate is camera-ready.
- Get consent from staff and customers who will be recognisable. Secure permission for any review screenshot or quote.
- Prepare a shot list, props and clean plates. Capture each hero dish in a vertical wide, medium and close-up shot, plus a still photo.

### While filming
- Record vertical 9:16 clips, generally 2–4 seconds each; hold the final hero shot for at least 2 seconds. Capture extra takes of the grill, steam, sauce and finished dish.
- Use real kitchen sounds (sizzle, chopping, serving) where clear; add music only after filming and use platform-cleared audio. Avoid staged customer reactions.
- Film enough coverage for the Reel and that day’s Stories in the same session. Keep brand text legible and consistent.

### Before publishing
- Check spelling, address, menu availability, captions, tags, audio rights, consent and review accuracy.
- Add captions/subtitles to spoken video. Use readable contrast and avoid covering the food with text.
- Post the planned Story prompt, then check and respond to comments. Save useful audience answers for future content.
